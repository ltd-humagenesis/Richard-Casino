02Information we process

The Game and its integrated SDKs process the technical information needed for notifications and install attribution:

  • Device and app information: device model, Android version, app and SDK versions, language, time zone, network information, IP address and installation identifiers.
  • Push notification information: Firebase registration tokens and installation IDs, OneSignal user and subscription identifiers, notification permission and subscription status, and notification delivery or interaction records.
  • Installation information: install and first-launch timestamps, AppsFlyer identifiers, install-referrer and source information, and an Android advertising identifier where available, permitted and used by the configured SDK. These identifiers can qualify as personal data even when they do not reveal your name.
  • SDK session information: app opens, session times and similar technical activity recorded by the notification and install-attribution SDKs for their operation.
  • Information you send us: your email address, message content and any information you choose to provide when contacting support or exercising privacy rights.

Information comes directly from you, automatically from the Game and SDKs, and from install-referrer or attribution sources. An IP address may reveal an approximate country or region. We do not request precise GPS location, access to contacts or photos, or identity documents as part of ordinary Game use. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information in support messages.
